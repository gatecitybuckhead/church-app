Put us on your home screen

It takes about ten seconds, and afterwards GateCity opens like any other app — its own icon, full screen, no browser bar. There's nothing to download and no account to make.

Open this page in Safari It has to be Safari — Chrome on iPhone can't add to the home screen. If you're reading this in another app, tap the ••• menu and choose “Open in Safari”.

Tap the Share button The square with an arrow coming out of it — at the bottom of the screen on an iPhone, at the top on an iPad.

Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen” You may need to scroll a little way down the list of options.

Tap “Add” The GateCity icon appears on your home screen. That's it.

Open this page in Chrome

Tap the ⋮ menu, top right

Tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen” Chrome sometimes offers this as a banner at the bottom of the screen instead — either one works.

Trouble with it? Grab any of us on a Sunday and we'll set it up on your phone in a moment.
